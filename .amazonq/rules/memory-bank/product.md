# Product Overview

## Project Purpose
EduSync Myanmar is a comprehensive school management system built with Flutter, designed to streamline educational administration for schools in Myanmar. The application provides a unified platform for managing students, staff, classes, exams, finances, and communication between administrators, teachers, parents, and donors.

## Key Features

### Multi-Role Support
- **Admin**: Complete school management including user management, staff/student administration, class scheduling, exam management, financial oversight, and announcements
- **Teacher**: Attendance marking, lesson planning, marks entry, student management, and timetable viewing
- **Parent**: Child attendance tracking, schedule viewing, exam results, announcements, and daily reports
- **Manager**: School-level oversight and management capabilities
- **Donator**: Donation tracking and contribution management

### Core Capabilities

#### Academic Management
- Student and staff management with comprehensive profiles
- Class organization and timetable scheduling
- Exam creation, scheduling, and management with templates
- Marks entry with approval workflows
- Grade and subject management
- Report card generation and analytics
- Student performance tracking and analytics

#### Financial Management
- Fee structure management and payment tracking
- Income and expense management with categorization
- Donation management
- Salary payment processing
- Financial reports (Profit/Loss, Cash Flow, Fee Collection)
- Enhanced finance dashboard with analytics

#### Communication & Engagement
- School-wide announcements system
- In-app notifications
- Custom forms creation and response collection
- Daily reports for parents

#### Attendance & Scheduling
- Student and staff attendance tracking
- Attendance summary reports with filtering
- Lesson plan management
- Timetable management and viewing

#### Platform Support
- Cross-platform: Android, iOS, Windows, Linux, macOS, Web
- Responsive design with platform-adaptive screens
- Desktop-optimized interfaces for administrative tasks
- Mobile-first design for on-the-go access

## Target Users

### Primary Users
- **School Administrators**: Managing all aspects of school operations
- **Teachers**: Daily classroom activities, attendance, and assessment
- **Parents**: Monitoring children's academic progress and school activities
- **School Managers**: Oversight and strategic management
- **Donors**: Contributing to school development and tracking donations

### Use Cases
- Complete school digitalization from paper-based systems
- Real-time attendance tracking and reporting
- Streamlined exam management and result publication
- Transparent financial management and reporting
- Enhanced parent-teacher-school communication
- Multi-school management support
- Offline-capable operations with sync functionality
- Localized support for Myanmar (Burmese and English)

## Value Proposition
EduSync Myanmar eliminates manual administrative overhead, provides real-time insights into school operations, enhances communication between stakeholders, and enables data-driven decision making for educational institutions in Myanmar.
